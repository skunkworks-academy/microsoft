Train for the consultant’s decisions

Use this guide after gaining basic accounting and ERP experience. You will configure a safe training company, explain posting behaviour and diagnose real business process problems.

Before you begin

  • Familiarity with double-entry accounting, purchasing, sales and inventory.
  • Access to an authorised Business Central sandbox or instructor-provided environment. The guide does not provision a tenant or include licences.
  • An estimated 30 hours across reading, sandbox practice and review. Adjust to your experience; this is a planning estimate.
  • English study content. Use Microsoft’s exam accommodations process if you need changes to the actual exam experience.

What you will demonstrate

  • Build a company with controlled access, dimensions and approvals.
  • Configure ledgers, posting groups, bank accounts, receivables, payables and fixed assets.
  • Choose item, customer, vendor and pricing settings that meet business rules.
  • Process and correct trade, inventory, journal and cash transactions with a clear audit trail.
  • Interpret a weighted mock result and select evidence-based revision tasks.

What this simulation matches

The standard timed session lasts 100 minutes, matching the duration currently stated by Microsoft. You practise scenario-based single-answer, multiple-answer and sequence questions, with review flags and a question navigator.

Microsoft does not publish its live item pool, exact item count per sitting, or item selection algorithm. These 50-question forms use Academy-authored questions and published domain ranges. They do not reproduce Microsoft exam content, Pearson VUE’s interface, interactive labs or locked case-study sections. Use the official exam sandbox to experience those controls.
