Plan · Practise · Reflect

Your route to exam readiness

Plan about 30 hours over four weeks, then extend practice where the evidence is weak. The weekly times include reading, existing labs, retrieval practice and the mock. These are estimates, not a completion guarantee.

WeekFocusEvidence and time
1Modules 1–2: company, security, core setup, dimensions, workflow.Configuration workbook and permission test · 7 hours.
2Modules 3–4: financial configuration and ledger relationships.Posting matrix, deferral example, fixed asset setup · 8 hours.
3Modules 5–7: master data, trade documents, inventory and UI investigation.Purchase-to-pay and order-to-cash traces · 8 hours.
4Module 8, capstone, timed mock and targeted rework.Reconciliation, mock review and remediation log · 7 hours.

Use a repeatable revision loop

  1. Before reading, explain the business rule in your own words.
  2. Recreate the worked example in a sandbox and predict the ledger entries before posting.
  3. Retrieve the rule the next day without looking at the notes.
  4. Take an untimed practice session. Record the concept behind each error.
  5. Complete a timed mock. Review both wrong answers and correct guesses.
  6. Rework the related lab, then explain why each distractor fails the requirement.

Capstone: launch a small distributor

Use a disposable company called Academy Distribution. The company buys items, sells to two customers, tracks two departments, takes a customer prepayment and reconciles its bank. Use synthetic amounts and local tax settings supplied by your instructor. Do not invent statutory tax treatment.

  1. Create the company, number series and two test roles. Demonstrate that a sales user cannot change financial setup.
  2. Configure chart of accounts, posting groups, two department values and a blocked combination. Explain how a missing matrix cell prevents posting.
  3. Create one vendor, two customers, two items and a fixed asset. Record payment terms, costing choice, unit conversions and a depreciation book.
  4. Receive 10 items at 100 currency units each and invoice them. Trace item quantity, value entries, vendor balance and G/L impact.
  5. Sell and ship 3 items at 160 each, then invoice. Explain why quantity and value evidence come from different entries. Exclude tax for this simplified example.
  6. Process a customer receipt of 480, apply it to the invoice and reconcile it against a synthetic bank statement. Resolve a bank fee separately.
  7. Record a deferral and fixed asset acquisition. Preview the depreciation and discuss a disposal rather than posting an unreviewed production transaction.
  8. Correct one permitted error using the supported reversal or correction action. Preserve the original audit trail and explain transaction restrictions.
  9. Export a mock review and prepare a one-page plan for the two weakest objectives.

Evidence and rubric

This is a self-assessed practice capstone. An instructor may review your workbook separately. No evidence or customer data is uploaded by this guide.

CriterionWeightObservable acceptance evidence
Company and access controls20%Company setup record, allowed and denied role test, workflow trace.
Financial configuration30%Posting matrix, dimensional control, documented AP/AR/asset settings.
Trade and inventory25%Linked receipt, invoice, shipment and inventory ledger traces with reconciled quantities.
Cash and correction20%Applied receipt, reconciled bank and supported correction with before/after evidence.
Explanation and revision5%Reasoned configuration choices and two specific remediation actions.

Score each criterion 0 (absent), 1 (incomplete), 2 (works with unexplained gaps), 3 (works and reconciles) or 4 (works, reconciles and is clearly justified). Multiply each weight by score ÷ 4. Aim for 80% and no failed access-control or reconciliation evidence. This is an Academy study benchmark.

Exam-day preparation

  • Recheck the current study guide, exam duration and appointment instructions before booking.
  • Use the official sandbox to practise question controls and section behaviour.
  • For the 50-question mock, budget about two minutes per question on average. Watch elapsed time and return to flagged questions.
  • Read the entire requirement, separate setup decisions from transaction processing, and respect “choose N” instructions.
  • Read Microsoft’s current Learn-resource policy and actual exam instructions. Our documentation link opens a separate tab; it does not emulate the restricted exam browser.
  • Ask Microsoft for approved accommodations when required. The untimed Academy mode is available for flexible study.

Cleanup and data care

Export synthetic evidence if required, then remove the disposable company only after checking your environment and approval. Clear browser progress and attempt history on shared devices. No student names, email addresses, tenant credentials or customer records are requested by the mock.
