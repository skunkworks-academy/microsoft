Use a training environment and the credentials provided by your instructor or lab provider.

  • Familiarity with business processes and basic accounting helps you follow the scenarios.
  • Complete the environment validation before creating or changing company data.
  • Work through the labs in order; later exercises build on earlier setup.

Your learning path

From company setup to daily operations.

Configure the business

Create companies and user profiles, migrate master data, set up approvals and explore Copilot.

Establish financial controls

Configure accounts, journals, dimensions, cash management, financial reporting and accounting periods.

Run connected processes

Set up purchasing, receivables, inventory and sales, then process transactions and reconcile balances.
